How to play

The goal

You run a mining facility. Your GPUs produce hash power, and the reward pool pays out 3% of itself every day, split between all miners by their share of the network's hash power. More effective hash means a bigger share.

Getting started

Connect your wallet on BNB Smart Chain and buy a mining license for 3 USDT (one per wallet). It gives you a starter set: one Lv1 GPU, a 2-slot rig, a 300 W PSU, a home room, 500 W of city grid power, a desk fan and ADSL internet. 99% of the license fee goes into the reward pool.

What is a Sat?

Rewards are counted in Sat, like Bitcoin. 1 Sat = 0.00000001 USDT, so 100,000,000 Sat = 1 USDT. Rewards build up every second and you claim them whenever you like.

Wear fee and repairUSDTOnce you have earned back what you spent, a wear fee starts at 0% and rises 1% per day, up to 90%. It is only taken from profit: the part of a claim that gives you back your own money is always fee-free. The fee goes back to the reward pool. The claim screen always shows the fee before you claim. Repair resets the fee to 0%; it first claims your mined Sat at the current fee, so it cannot be used to skip it. Repair costs 50% of the current fee applied to your total profit so far, so it gets more expensive the more you have earned. Repair costs do not count toward break-even, so the fee starts climbing again right away. 99% of the repair cost goes to the pool.

How to grow

  1. Fill your free slots with GPUs and upgrade them.
  2. When a limit is hit, the Capacity box tells you which one and what to buy.
  3. Upgrade power (PSU and electricity) before high-level GPUs, since they draw more watts.
  4. Keep heat under your cooling capacity, or you lose hash.
  5. Claim your Sat and reinvest, or take it out.
  6. Once the wear fee starts, claim often: the fee is taken at the rate on the day you claim.
  7. Remember to keep some CD1 aside for the future. Upgrade prices are fixed in CD1, so when the CD1 price rises, your upgrades cost more in dollars. A simple habit: put 50% of your CD1 into the game and keep 50%. If the price goes up, the CD1 you kept gains value and offsets your costs.

Where your payments go

99% of every USDT payment goes straight into the reward pool; 1% goes to the game owner. CD1 payments stay in the contract, and the USDT dividends that CD1 earns also feed the pool. The owner can add CD1 Exchange revenue, token income, owner support and marketing funds, and anyone can add a community donation. Every inflow is shown under its own name in the Reward pool box.

Returns are not guaranteed

What you earn depends on how many new miners join and on CD1 dividends. Miners who join early usually earn more. When fewer new miners join, late miners may not earn back what they spent. The wear fee after break-even sends part of the earliest miners' earnings back to the pool for everyone. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

Good to know

Your production is a live estimate. When new miners join, the network grows and your share gets smaller; when the pool grows, everyone earns more. Everything happens on chain: the contract is verified on BscScan and has no admin keys.
